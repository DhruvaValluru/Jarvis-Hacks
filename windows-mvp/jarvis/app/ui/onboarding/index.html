    Bring your old settings

    Alfred found settings from the console version in . Import them and you won’t have to paste your keys again.

    If you used Alfred from the console before, choose its windows-mvp\.env file to import your settings and keys.

    SettingValue

    Alfred’s keys and tokens go to Windows Credential Manager, protected by your Windows sign-in, and are shown here only by their last few characters. Other settings that look secret (marked “not imported”) are left out. The old file stays as it is.

    Connect Alfred to Claude

    Alfred uses Claude, by Anthropic, to understand what you ask and work out the steps. It needs an API key: in the Anthropic Console, open API keys and create one.

    Open the Anthropic Console

    Used Alfred from the console before? instead.

    Checking is free: Alfred asks Anthropic for its list of models. If you skip this, Alfred only understands simple built-in commands like “open notepad” or “volume 30”. You can add a key later in Settings.

    You stay in control

    Using your mouse and keyboard

    When none of Alfred’s built-in tools can do something, Alfred can look at your screen and use the mouse and keyboard, the way you would. While it does, screenshots of your screen go to Anthropic so Claude can see what to click.

    Alfred always asks before it sends, deletes, buys or posts anything, and it never types passwords.

    Stopping Alfred

    Each of these stops whatever Alfred is doing straight away and lets go of the mouse and keyboard:

    The STOP buttonTop right of Alfred’s window, always there.
    The tray iconAlfred’s icon by the clock: Stop everything.
    Ctrl+Alt+BackspaceFrom any app.
    /stop in TelegramOnce you set up Telegram.

    While Alfred moves the mouse by itself, pushing your mouse into the top-left corner of the screen stops it too.

    Voice mode and voice notes Optional

    With an OpenAI API key you can talk with Alfred like on a phone call: press the microphone next to Send. Alfred also understands the voice notes you send it in Telegram and can answer with a spoken reply.

    With this key, your Telegram voice notes and the text of Alfred’s spoken replies go to OpenAI, and so does your microphone audio while voice mode is on. Nothing else does.

    Your other PCs Optional

    Alfred can work across your PCs: open something on your laptop while you sit at your desktop, or copy a file from one to the other. Every PC needs the same pairing code.

    1This is my first PC Make a pairing code here, then paste it on your other PCs.
    +Join my other PCs Paste the pairing code from your first PC.

    Your pairing code. Copy it now: Alfred doesn’t show it again.

    Keep it secret. Your PCs trust anyone who has it (anything risky still needs your OK at the PC it happens on).

    This PC

    When this is on, your other PCs with the same pairing code can open apps on this PC, read and type into its windows, and copy files to and from it. Anything risky (sending, deleting, buying, posting, or writing a file) still needs your OK here, at this PC.

    Windows Firewall may ask whether Alfred may use the network. Allow it on private networks only.
